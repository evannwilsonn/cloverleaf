Cloverleaf

Every 30 minutes a scheduled job records 12 seconds of video from 20 live Caltrans cameras on Bay Area freeways and counts the vehicles with an object detector. A capture only counts once it passes the quality checks.

Why captures were not counted

Each capture goes through the checks in order and is labelled by the first one it fails. Only captures that pass all of them feed the traffic numbers.

How accurate the counting is

Frames saved from the collector were counted by hand and compared with what the detector found in the same frame, with the collector's own settings. In busy frames both count only the near part of the picture, where a person can count reliably. The dbt build fails if daylight accuracy drops below its gate.

How it is built

  • CollectGitHub Actions, every 30 min: OpenCV reads each HLS stream, YOLOv8s + ByteTrack count and track
  • LandCaptures and NWS weather as JSON lines on a data branch, loaded raw into DuckDB or Snowflake
  • Modeldbt: staging, quality rules, core facts, reporting marts, with data tests
  • CheckHand-labelled frames vs detector counts, split by daylight and dark
  • PublishThis page, exported from the reporting marts with a current still per camera
