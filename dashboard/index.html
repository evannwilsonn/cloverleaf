Video: Caltrans District 4 CCTV live streams. Weather: National Weather Service station observations. Vehicles are detected and tracked with YOLOv8s + ByteTrack on a 12-second clip per camera; a vehicle counts as moving when it is tracked for 3+ frames and crosses at least 4% of the frame. Congestion compares each camera's speed to its own free-flow speed, because camera angles make raw speeds incomparable between cameras. Built with dbt on Snowflake · github.com/evannwilsonn/cloverleaf
